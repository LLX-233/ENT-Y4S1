## Participant context

P01 studies away from home. The participant's parents travel
frequently, creating periods with no fixed person responsible
for plant care.

## D1 evidence supported

- EV-W2-01: When the participant and parents are away, there is
  no reliable person responsible for plant care.
- EV-W2-02: Automated care must include visible readings, manual
  confirmation, safety limits, an emergency stop and human override.

## Evidence boundary

This is one self-reported user case. It supports early problem and
safety-requirement validation, not market-wide conclusions.
