# Week 2 Problem Re-Validation

Date: 2026-09-19

This folder records anonymised Week 2 user-interview evidence
for GreenSight AI.

## Interview evidence

P01: The participant studies away from home.
The participant's parents travel frequently, creating periods
with no fixed person responsible for plant care.

P02: The participant is completing a six-month internship.
The participant's plants remain in a former dormitory, so the
participant cannot physically check or care for them.

## D1 evidence mapping

EV-W2-01: P01 identified a responsibility gap when all relevant
people are away.

EV-W2-02: P01 required visible sensor readings, manual confirmation,
safety limits, an emergency stop and human override.

EV-W2-03: P02 explained that reminders cannot solve a six-month
absence when the user cannot physically act.

EV-W2-04: P02 required remote monitoring, limited watering,
failure alerts and handover to a trusted person. Purchase interest
depends on usefulness after the long-absence period.

## Evidence boundary

These are two individual interview cases, not representative market
evidence. Further validation is needed with busy urban professionals
and office plant coordinators.

## Privacy

Participant codes are used to protect privacy. Recording links are
submitted in the D1 Evidence Register. Recordings, personal details
and raw interview materials are not stored in this public repository.
