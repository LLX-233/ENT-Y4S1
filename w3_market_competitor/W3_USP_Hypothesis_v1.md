# Green Sight AI — W3 USP Hypothesis v1

**Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Purpose:** ENT303TC S1 W3 Market and Competitor Analysis

## Target user

Busy urban indoor pot-plant owners, initially university students in dorms and early-career professionals living alone or renting, who may be away during classes, work or short travel.

## USP hypothesis

For this target user, Green Sight AI is being developed as a desk-ready plant-care system that turns multiple sensor readings into a clear care recommendation and a physical care action only after user confirmation and within pre-set safety limits.

Unlike Xiaomi Flower Care, which monitors plant conditions and provides care references but leaves the owner to decide and carry out watering or lighting themselves, Green Sight AI aims to close the gap between knowing that a plant needs care and carrying out an agreed care action.

## Why this may matter to users

The user should be able to see the reason for a recommendation, approve or refuse the proposed action, and retain control through limits and alerts. The intended benefit is fewer missed care events without asking users to accept unrestricted automatic watering.

## What would disprove the hypothesis

The hypothesis would be weakened if target users say that Xiaomi Flower Care, phone reminders or timer irrigators already solve the problem well enough.

It would also be weakened if users do not value an explanation before action, or if concerns about leakage, noise, appearance, electrical safety, setup effort or price outweigh the proposed benefit.

## Next validation action

In Week 4, the team will show eight target users a like-for-like comparison between Xiaomi Flower Care and the Green Sight AI concept. Each participant will be asked to explain the difference between monitoring-only and user-approved, safety-limited action; identify which option they would prefer to trial; and explain the main reason for that choice.

The hypothesis will be revised if fewer than six participants can explain the difference without prompting, or if most participants judge existing monitoring, reminders or timer irrigation to be sufficient.

## Evidence boundary

The current prototype includes sensors and basic actuators. Its integrated multi-sensor decision process, approval flow, safety limits and user value have not yet been validated. This document therefore describes a hypothesis under development, not a mature AI system, proven autonomous plant care or a commercially ready product.
